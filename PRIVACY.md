# Privacy Policy

Garage's privacy policy is published at
**<https://garagerag.app/support/privacy-policy.html>**. Its source is
[`docs/support/privacy-policy.md`](docs/support/privacy-policy.md), and that file is the only copy:
edit it there.

In short, Garage is local-first. It collects no data, has no analytics or telemetry, and sends your
content only to model servers you configure. Messages and Mail never leave your Mac. The policy lists
every network request the app makes: the model catalog, the model downloads you choose, and update
checks in the Developer ID build.
